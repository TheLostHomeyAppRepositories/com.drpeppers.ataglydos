Control your Atag Lydos Hybrid heat pump water heater from Homey.

Set the desired water temperature, switch between the i-Memory, Green, Program and Boost modes, turn the water heater on or off, and see the current water temperature, whether it is heating and how many showers are available.

The app uses the Ariston NET cloud (Atag is part of the Ariston group), so the water heater must be connected through Wi-Fi and working in the Atag app. Log in with the same e-mail address and password.

This is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with Atag or Ariston.
